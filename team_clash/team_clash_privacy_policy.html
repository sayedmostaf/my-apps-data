Welcome to Team Clash

Your privacy is our priority. At Buniasoft Inc., we build competitive experiences that respect your data. This policy explains how we handle your information in the Team Clash mobile application.

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this document. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

📊

Information Collection

We collect only the minimum data necessary to facilitate high-performance multiplayer gameplay:

  • Device Identity: Model, operating system, and hardware identifiers to optimize game stability and settings.
  • Player Identity: The nickname and avatar you choose to represent yourself in game rooms.
  • Competitive Data: Real-time scores, team selections, and match results to maintain fair play and leaderboards.
⚡

Data Processing

We process your data through secure, high-speed channels for the following purposes:

  • Real-time Sync: Keeping match states updated across all participants simultaneously.
  • Matchmaking: Connecting you with players and managing game room logic.
  • Support: Diagnosing technical issues and improving app performance via anonymous crash reports.
🔗

Infrastructure Partners

We leverage industry-leading infrastructure to power Team Clash:

  • Supabase: Our primary backend for real-time data sync and encrypted storage.
  • Google Play Services: For application distribution, secure updates, and platform features.
Encrypted Sync Supabase DB Google Core
🛡️

Data Retention & Deletion

We believe in "Ephemeral Data" principles. Game room data (scores and participant names) is typically cleared once a session is completed and inactive for a prolonged period.

If you wish to request the deletion of any persistent data associated with your device ID, please contact our support team below.

⚖️

Your Global Rights

Regardless of your location, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectify: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: Request that we delete your data under certain conditions.
👶

Children's Safety

Team Clash is safe for all audiences, but we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will purge it from our systems immediately.
