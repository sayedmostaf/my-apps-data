1. Introduction

Blockrix is a premium, retro-inspired puzzle game designed for pure entertainment. We believe your gaming experience should be private and uninterrupted. This policy explains why we don't collect your data—because we don't need it.

🚫

2. What We NEVER Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Geographic location or IP addresses
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Gameplay telemetry or usage statistics
  • Social media profiles or contact lists
Blockrix is an "Offline-First" application. It does not communicate with any external servers during gameplay.
💾

3. Local Data Storage

To provide a consistent experience, the following data is saved locally on your device using encrypted local storage (SharedPreferences/SQLite):

  • High scores and game progress
  • Custom settings (Sound, Vibration, Theme)
  • Achievement status and unlocks
This data stays on your device. We have no access to it. Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all local game data.

4. Minimal Permissions

Blockrix requires zero sensitive permissions. We will never ask for access to your:

  • Camera or Microphone
  • Contacts or SMS
  • Storage Photos/Media (except for saving game state)
  • Network/Internet access
🛡️

5. Third-Party Services

Unlike most modern games, Blockrix contains no third-party SDKs. This means:

  • No Google Analytics or Firebase Tracking
  • No AdMob or advertising networks
  • No Crashlytics (we strive for stability without spying)
👶

6. Children's Privacy

Blockrix is safe for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, we are fully compliant with COPPA and global privacy standards regarding children.
